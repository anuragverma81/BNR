THE STORY

What is BNR?

BNR - Beyond Normal Reality - is an independent, community-inspired meme token prototype.

Our Vision

BNR explores how a community can use blockchain technology as a learning and creativity experiment.The project begins with a website and a test token on Solana Devnet.

Our approach

Transparency,responsible experimentation and community participation are the foundation of this prototype.

Important distinction

BNR is independent. It is not affiliated with Dogecoin, its creators, or the Solana organization.
